Troop artwork belongs to Scorewarrior, the makers of Total Battle.

The 33 troops game01master's calculator covers use its icons. The other 65
(every other tier I-IX guardsman, specialist, monster and engineer) were
cropped to 96px webp from the troop cards on totalbattlewiki.com, an unofficial
player wiki, which also supplied their strength, health and features.

The 45 mercenaries added in Nov 2026 are close-ups from the card art on
totalbattlewiki.com/troops/mercenaries/, set in the mercenary icon frame taken
from game01master's Wyvern icon (so that frame falls under its licence below).
Epic Monster Hunter V-VII reuse the Superior Epic Monster Hunter's icon.

game01master's icons come from its Citadel Calculator
(https://github.com/game01master/tb-citadel-calculator), converted to 96px webp.
Tools → Citadel also uses its troop-sizing method for Elven 25 and 30.
