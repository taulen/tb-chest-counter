# Guards levels and Gold Pass (from the game)

Both come straight from the game protocol since Nov 2026. Nothing is estimated and nothing is entered by hand.

- **The guards level is the Guardsmen tier from the member's own research.** Every member's city (message 601) is read once a game day, and its research levels go into `game_player_techs`, with a `game_player_tech_history` row for every change. [troop-tiers.ts](../src/game-protocol/troop-tiers.ts) turns research into tiers: the highest Guardsmen unit unlocked (Archers I … VII are G1–G7; the "Guardsmen II" tree restarts at I, so Smiter I/II are G8/G9). [member-tiers.ts](../src/data/member-tiers.ts) does this for every linked member (S/E/M and crypt level too). [guards-repo.ts](../src/data/repositories/guards-repo.ts) serves the G tier under the old interfaces (`getGuardsSummaries`, `getGuardsLevels`).
- **No research read, no level.** A member who isn't linked to a game player, or whose city hasn't been read yet, has `null`. Nothing falls back to a guess. Tin Man targets and the Ancient planner treat unknown as G7, as before.
- **Level-ups are dated between two reads.** Replaying the unlock techs' history gives every tier change after the member's **first** research read. `by` is the game day of the read that saw it; `after` is the read before it (`game_city_snapshots`). A tier held at the first read was reached at some unknown time before it. It is shown as "since before <first read>", never as a level-up. The Research tab's tier cards show this for every family. The Performance page's Tin Man tiers use the level on each run's day (`guardsLevelOn`).
- **Old = not read for 7 game days** (`GUARDS_STALE_DAYS`, [guards-levels.ts](../src/config/guards-levels.ts)). Cities are read daily, so a week without a read means the game stopped returning that city.
- **Gold Pass is the flag on the player's game record**: message 402 field 22, `game_players.gold_pass`, read every scan. `game_player_history` gets a row whenever it changes ([gold-pass-repo.ts](../src/data/repositories/gold-pass-repo.ts)). "Now" is today's flag. For any other window, a member counts when a scan inside it saw their flag on: the Events → Triumphal tab's cycles, a "previous" GP marker, and Analytics' "last cycle". That is a history row inside the window with the flag on, or no row inside it at all, the flag on before it, and the player read again since the window opened (`game_players.last_seen`). **The flag from before a window never counts on its own.** The game switches every pass off at the Triumphal reset, and the first scan after it writes the "off" row. Carrying the flag in counted last cycle's holders and players who had left before the cycle: 105 holders on Nov 21 2026 against the game's 92. The Triumphal card counts the clan as it is now, out of its members ("92 of 98"), the same figure as Analytics. Holders aren't a subset of the participants, since a holder may not have collected a Union Chest yet. **A window before the clan's game history starts has no answer** (`basis: 'none'`, no count shown). One that starts before it is `partial`. Analytics shows "last cycle" only when the history covers the whole cycle; a part-covered count would read as a real drop.
- **Chat levels** (the retired Admin → Levels from chat, [member-levels.md](member-levels.md)): their rows are kept as what the player said; nothing reads them. The Dragon level, the one letter research doesn't have, comes from the game too since Nov 2026 (city field 26).

## Retired: the donation estimate (Oct–Nov 2026)

Before the protocol switch-over the game showed neither value anywhere the OCR scanner could read, so both were inferred.

- **Guards level** came from donations. Omen Essence lines are exact multiples of a per-level unit (G7 11,000, G8 14,300, G9 18,600; a full donation is 70 units). Scientific Tractates come in one fixed amount per level (15M G7, 58M G8, 76M G9). The best non-decreasing level path through each member's dated donations was computed on every read. Admins could pin a level by hand (`member_guards_reports`, v75), and the chat import wrote one too. Checked against ChestTracker's hand-entered levels, it agreed for 116 of 131 members.
- **Gold Pass** was a Union Chest plus an exact 250,000 Scientific Tractates line in the same Triumphal cycle. A pass-reward Union Chest donates exactly that when opened.

The estimator (`guards-estimator.ts`), the essence/tractate ladder (`guards-ladder.ts`) and the admin entry routes are removed. `member_guards_reports` stays in the schema, unread. The research tier is exact, and the game's flag is the game's own answer, so neither needs the inference. The first prod comparison (Nov 17 2026, clan 2) had 16 game flags against 12 inferred holders.
