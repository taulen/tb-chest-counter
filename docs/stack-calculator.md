# Stack calculator (Tools → Stacks)

Sizes every stack of a march from one base stack, so the enemy wears down the cheap stacks first and reaches the valuable ones last. Mostly for epic monsters, crypts and other PvE. The rule is in [stack-calc.js](../src/web/public/lib/stack-calc.js); the page is [stacks-tool.js](../src/web/public/pages/stacks-tool.js). Saved armies are per account: `stack_presets` (v97) in [stack-preset-repo.ts](../src/data/repositories/stack-preset-repo.ts), `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/tools/stack-presets`. Troop levels and the picker's "Show top N levels" filter are the Citadel tab's (`citadel_levels` incl. `span`, `/api/tools/citadel-levels`, [troop-levels.js](../src/web/public/lib/troop-levels.js)); one set per account serves both tabs. The window rule is in [citadel-simulator.md](citadel-simulator.md) ("Your levels").

## Where the rule comes from

It is a port of [Kaiculator](https://kaiculator.kaikaiju.com/) v0.71.5 ("estimated" mode), read from its published bundle in Nov 2026. [stack-calc.test.ts](../tests/web/stack-calc.test.ts) holds 19 armies entered on Kaiculator itself, driven headlessly through its own saved-settings format, and checks every count. Those cases cover plain stacks, specialists, health bonuses, monsters and mercenaries, siege, both dominance modes, mercenaries growing back, "Find it for me", tier multiplier 2 and the epic hunter, with and without its own health bonus. Change a rule and a count stops matching. To re-probe after a Kaiculator release, load the page in Playwright (system Edge), write `localStorage.uiSettings` (`{ referenceCount, selected: ['#<checkbox id>'], healthBonuses: [{ types: ['Flying'], value: 100 }], … }`), call `loadSettings()` and `updateTroops()`, and read each `#<id>-count`'s `data-count`.

The arithmetic follows Kaiculator's exactly: it multiplies by `1 / (1 + bonus)` and adds a bonus as `base / mult − base`. With plain division, one case in 14 came out 0.4% off, because a count landed on a rounding edge.

## The rule

- **Loss order = stack health, most first.** An enemy stack hits the stack it can hurt most; when it can finish any of them, that's the one with the most health. Stacks with exactly equal health fall as a group ("4–6").
- **Base stack:** the selected guardsman/specialist with the highest unit health. On ties, guardsmen win, then ranged → melee → mounted → flying (Kaiculator's list order). Its count is what the player sets, or what Fill finds.
- **Guardsmen and specialists:** `count = tierMultiplier^(baseTier − tier) × (TYPE[type] ÷ TYPE[baseType]) × r ÷ (1 + bonus)`, at least 1. Here TYPE is flying 1, mounted 10, melee/ranged 20: one flyer has the health of 10 riders or 20 foot soldiers in the same tier. Vultures (flying specialists below tier 8) count as melee. The raw size `r` comes from a binary search, so the base stack lands on the wanted count. With the default 1.9 against the ~1.8× unit health per tier, each tier lower carries ~5% more health. **Specialists first** (default on): a specialist gets `floor(maxGuardsHealthOfItsTier × 1.01 ÷ unitHealth) + 1` units, so it falls before the guardsmen of its tier.
- **Monsters and mercenaries:** walked in Kaiculator's processing order: strength ÷ health rounded, then strength, then tier, all ascending. Each gets `floor(cap ÷ hp) × 1/(1+bonus)` units; the cap starts at 97% of the base stack's health, and each stack sized becomes the next one's cap. So the weakest gets the biggest stack and the strongest falls last. **Epic hunter** option: the Epic Monster Hunter's +1000% epic strength counts in that order, so it sorts last and keeps the smallest stack.
- **Dominance over the limit** (only when a limit is entered): *keep the counts* only warns; *scale* multiplies every monster count by limit ÷ needed; *whole stacks* keeps monsters in processing order while they fit and drops the rest. When monsters were cut and an authority limit is entered, mercenaries grow back towards a full stack (97% of base), all by the same share, as far as the authority left allows.
- **Siege engines (decoys):** `ceil(biggestStackHealth × scale ÷ hp)`, where `scale = (1.15 + (baseTier − bestEngineerTier) × baseTier/9 × 0.25) × siegeMultiplier`. Ariel is a tier-9 siege mercenary and only sets the tier when no engineer is picked. Siege health bonuses are ignored, as in Kaiculator, and the siege multiplier is the knob instead. The page hides the box on siege rows for that reason.
- **Costs** (Kaiculator's table): guardsmen/specialists take 1 leadership, mounted 2, Griffins/Corax/Royal Lions 20 (Vultures 1). Siege engines take 10. Monster dominance and mercenary authority are per troop (`MONSTER_DOMINANCE`, `MERCENARIES`). Revival gold is 4 × leadership for trained troops, 40 per siege engine and 16 × dominance per monster.
- **Revival cost** = Σ floor(0.9 × count) × revival gold ÷ the Temple reduction (level 1–45, 1.04…5.91).
- **Fill my leadership:** Kaiculator's "Find it for me". It sizes once at base 100, scales to the leadership, and then steps to the exact edge: the largest base whose whole army, siege included, fits. In Fill mode it runs again on every change.

## Deliberate differences from Kaiculator

- No limit entered = no limit. Kaiculator drops every monster when its over-limit handling is on and the dominance box is empty.
- Siege engines count in the revival cost; Kaiculator leaves them out of the displayed figure.
- Health bonuses are one number per troop, the total from its card, with Citadel's family links (Corax I/II…). Kaiculator takes per-attribute bonuses (Flying, Guardsman, Human…) and adds them up. The cases above were converted the same way.
  - This matters most for Epic Monster Hunters. They have no movement class, so their card bonus sits well below the guardsmen's: 914.3% against 1,555% on the user's account (Nov 2026). Filled in the usual way, with one Guardsman number, Kaiculator hands them the guardsmen's figure and plans ~40% fewer hunters than the real bonus gives (1,234 vs 2,014 in `emh-one-guardsman` / `emh-914-split`). The sizing is the same; only the input differs.
  - In the user's real Fenrir fight, 1,883 Superior hunters planned at their true 914% still fell early, even though the plan had them at #33 of 34. The suspected cause is not proven yet: the targeting tie-break measured for Citadel (a hit too small to finish a stack lands on whichever would hit back hardest) and Kaiculator's assumption that every hit finishes a stack.
- Not ported: battle-report calibration, sub-tier stacking, the power score and the "enemy stacks" attack assessment, profiles shared by URL.

## Mercenaries

53 mercenaries, in `TROOPS` (so the Citadel picker offers them too). They are every fighting merc on [totalbattlewiki.com/troops/mercenaries/](https://totalbattlewiki.com/troops/mercenaries/) (Nov 2026), with stats, "vs" features, authority, revival gold and card art. Grace and Pathfinder are left out: they are scouts and don't fight. Epic Monster Hunter V–VII come from Kaiculator's table and share the Superior hunter's icon. Each merc carries `mercTier` (the exchange tier, which the sizing uses for order and siege scale), `authority`, `revivalGold` and `epicPct`.

- Against Kaiculator's table, 49 match exactly. **Scorpion**: Kaiculator has 3,200 health, the wiki 32,000; 32,000 fits siege health = 6× strength, so Kaiculator's is a typo. **Sphynx and Shedu**: the wiki has one at tier VI, 75.6k/226.8k (authority 40). Kaiculator has a VI at 18.9k/56.7k (authority 10) and a VII at 136k/408k (authority 40). We use the wiki's, **unconfirmed**; an in-game card would settle it.
- Left out, with no art anywhere: Kaiculator's tier-VII Legionary/Arbalester/Chariot/Knight/Rhino Rider/Trailseeker, the bounty hunters, and the swarm mercs (Grim Stalker, Wasp-Man, Chitinous Defender, Combat Anteater, and their Leaders).
- Icons are built in the game's own mercenary style, to sit beside game01master's five in-game merc icons. The parchment frame, folded corner and green badge are lifted from its Wyvern icon. Parchment-coloured pixels connected to the icon's edge form the frame, and the badge's "II" is smoothed into a blank gem, because which numeral the game shows on other mercs is unknown. Inside the frame is a close-up (face or head) cut from the wiki's card art (900×1736). The crop box is hand-picked per merc: `{ top, left, size }` as fractions of the card, chosen on gridded contact sheets. The first, scenic crops (whole card, attention crop) looked nothing like the in-game icons the user knows.

## The page

- Three steps: troops (rows always in the troop list's order, under a heading per kind like the picker's, no dragging), size and limits (base count Fill/Manual, leadership/authority/dominance, Temple, Fine-tuning folded away), and the stacks in loss order, with the leadership/authority/dominance used against your limits and the revival cost.
- Leadership, Authority, Dominance and Temple are the game's words and go through `tGame()`, so they stay English until an official name is in a dictionary.
- The troop picker is [troop-picker.js](../src/web/public/lib/troop-picker.js), shared with Citadel; the March list reuses `marchList` from citadel-sim.js.
- The draft is kept in this browser (`tbcc.stacks.draft.v1`). Saved armies live on the server: at most 50 per account and 200 troops per army (`MAX_PRESET_ROWS`, above the whole troop list; it was 30, which refused real stack armies), with the same name rules as the citadel presets (`parsePresetName`).
