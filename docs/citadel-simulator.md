# Citadel simulator (Tools → Citadel)

Plays a citadel attack out strike by strike, for both coin flips, from the troops and bonuses a player enters. The rules and data are in [citadel-sim.js](../src/web/public/lib/citadel-sim.js); the page is [citadel-tool.js](../src/web/public/pages/citadel-tool.js). Saved armies and troop levels are per account: `citadel_presets` (v95) and `citadel_levels` (v96), both in [citadel-preset-repo.ts](../src/data/repositories/citadel-preset-repo.ts); `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/tools/citadel-presets`, `GET/PUT /api/tools/citadel-levels`.

## How the rules were obtained

Nothing here is guessed. Real battle reports (Nov 2026: Elven 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30) were decoded number by number. [citadel-sim.test.ts](../tests/web/citadel-sim.test.ts) replays 14 of them strike for strike, including the losses, so a changed rule fails the suite. Several were tests designed to tell two explanations apart.

The first army the simulator sized itself was then played for real (Elven 30, 8 stacks with Vultures and Battle Griffins, Nov 7 2026). The page predicted 65 Corax I lost if you open and 140 if the citadel does. The game took exactly 65 and 140, and the you-open report matched all 11 strikes. Both results are pinned. The same day, a Cursed 25 report (4 stacks, 10 strikes, 2 Corax I and 11 Battle Griffin VII lost) matched too, the first Cursed one.

- **Siege.** It only happens while the walls stand. Siege engines strike first (initiative +5 in a siege), then the walls (11), then everything else (10), which hits the walls. Rounds repeat until the walls fall. Walls do hit flying troops. Siege engines deal ×20 to fortifications on top of their "vs fortifications" feature. Units the walls kill are lost; the walls' wounds heal before the battle (5 Fire Phoenix I took 104.6M in total and lost none).
- **Turn order.** A coin flip decides who opens round 1. After that, the side that did NOT act last opens the next round; it is not a new flip. Within a round the sides alternate, each using its strongest stack that has not acted yet. When one side runs out, the other side's remaining stacks act back to back. Stack strength for ordering is units × strength × (1 + bonus); features play no part.
- **Targeting.** A stack hits the enemy it would deal the most damage to. Features ("vs mounted +667 %") are ADDED to the bonus, and the damage is capped at what the target has left. Ties go to the target that would hit back hardest. That last rule is why a Fire Phoenix picks the Bear (+50% vs elementals) over the Ent. It is also why game01master rounds DOWN: one Royal Lion I more and its plain damage beats the Centaur's 382.2M, so it hits the Ent instead.
- **Damage and losses.** Damage = units × strength × (1 + bonus + matching features). A stack's health is a pool; units alive = ceil(pool ÷ unit health), and a damaged stack strikes with those units. **Wounds stay through the whole battle, for every troop kind:** guardsmen (Corax I 211/114 to the unit), specialists (Royal Lion I), monsters (Fire Phoenix I), mercenaries (Wyvern) and siege engines (Catapult V). Counting each hit on its own fits none of the reports.
- **Luck** is not simulated: double damage and striking a second squad, both known troop bonuses. The tests replay them through `luck` so the reports match exactly. No report has shown a blocked strike. The only source for blocks is the wiki ("Block a Strike" in the Monsters Boost research, no details), and the user (an experienced player) had never heard of it, so the page doesn't mention it.

## Data

- **Troops: every tier I–IX** from [totalbattlewiki.com](https://totalbattlewiki.com)'s troop pages (Nov 2026): 32 guardsmen (Spearman/Archer/Rider I–V, Battle Griffin V–VII, Heavy Halberdier/Heavy Arbalester/Mounted Knight VI–VII, Punisher/Purifier/Smiter/Corax I–II), 24 specialists (Swordsman I–V, Deadshot/Lion Rider/Vulture V–VII, Heavy Knight VI–VII, Duelist/Legitimist/Whitemane/Royal Lion I–II), 28 monsters (tiers 3–9, four per tier; the wiki lists none at 1–2), 9 engineers (Catapult I–V, Siege Ballistae VI–VII, Josephine I–II), plus 53 mercenaries (every fighting merc on the wiki's mercenaries page, Nov 7 2026, and Kaiculator's Epic Monster Hunter V–VII; see [stack-calculator.md](stack-calculator.md#mercenaries)). Only the first five were checked in game. Scouts (Spy, Swift Jaeger, Panoptic) are left out: they don't fight. The wiki shows values rounded ("3.9k"), so new troops can be off in the last digit. The 33 game01master troops were checked against game cards and battle reports and agree with the wiki exactly. **Siege health is 6× strength** (the Siege Ballistae VII card reads 51,000; the wiki halves every siege engine). Josephine I, Ballistae VI and Catapults IV/V keep game01master's rounded card values, because the Catapult V battle replay uses 15,800.
- **Defenders:** every Elven and Cursed unit's card (stats, features, race) was checked in game. Counts per citadel come from in-game march screens. The wiki says Elven 10 has 200 Druids; the game has 100.
- **game01master's sizing** (Elven 25/30 only, `gmCounts`) reproduces its calculator exactly (621/26/166/779 for the reference army). Its Bear target is deliberately under the real 792M / 158.4M, so the Phoenix's damage stays below the Bear's health and it doesn't switch to the Ent. Its "first strike losses" counts only the dragon's opening hit; the simulator counts every hit.

## Automatic counts

`optimizeCounts` searches, for ANY citadel, the counts of the chosen troop types that win both coin flips with the fewest losses. **Monsters and mercenaries are never traded for cheaper losses** (`PROTECTED_GROUPS`): the fewest monster/mercenary losses decide first, and only then the other losses, weighed by each troop's base health; ties go to the smaller army. The user's rule: the first striker (and other guardsmen, specialists, siege) absorb losses, monsters and mercs should never die. On their Cursed 25 army (Ballistae, Corax I, Phoenix I, Royal Lion I, Griffin VII), a pure health-weighted score lost a Phoenix when the citadel struck first; the protected order finds 499/67/5/14/0, which loses only Corax I. It is a local search over each row's count. It tries the breakpoints where a stack's damage crosses a defender's health (the counts where its target can change) plus plain nudges. Starting points: every stack big enough to one-shot the biggest defender, game01master's counts, and the player's own counts. Every candidate must ALSO win with all bonuses 3% lower (`SAFETY`), and its losses are averaged over both, so it can't settle on a count that only works to the last unit.

On the user's Elven 30 army (Corax I, Phoenix I, Royal Lion I, Ballistae VII) it finds 94 / 174 Corax I against game01master's 132 / 211. It does that by leaving 41 Centaurs alive, whose weak strike lands harmlessly on the Ballistae. On Elven 25 it finds 21 / 37 against 26 / 43. With four flying cleanups added it matches game01master + cleanups (69 / 149).

## The page

- **What the player fills in:** the strength bonus for every troop, and the health bonus only where the page says "needed". A troop with no health counts as untouchable (`UNKNOWN_HEALTH_FACTOR`). That can only make it a likelier target, so if it still isn't hit, its health doesn't matter. If it is hit, the page asks for it and names the attacker. In game01master's mode the first striker always needs health, because its count depends on it.
- **Counts:** Auto (the search above, every citadel, the default), game01master (Elven 25/30, its exact sizing, roles by row order) or Manual. Worked-out counts are shown as results, not input boxes. The search runs 350 ms after the last keystroke.
- **Bonuses are never filled in for the player.** They change with every captain, artifact or research swap. The note says when they were typed or saved.
- **Bonus families** (`BONUS_FAMILIES`, from game01master's `getBonusGroup`): Corax I/II; Fire Phoenix I/II + Royal Lion I/II + Jago; Vulture V–VII; Battle Griffin V–VII + Warregal; Punisher/Heavy Halberdier/Spearman; Duelist/Heavy Knight/Swordsman. game01master keeps Phoenix and Royal Lion apart. They're merged here because the user's cards showed identical strength AND health for Phoenix I and Royal Lion I twice. A troop that joins the army takes its family's values. Typing a bonus fills the family's other boxes, **but only boxes that agreed (or were empty) and that the player hasn't typed in this visit.** Give a troop its own number and it stops following, now and after a reload, since it no longer agrees. A family that still agrees updates together for the next attack. The row says "bonuses shared with …" only while the rows agree. game01master links strength only; health is linked here too. Wyverns, Manticore, Ariel, the Epic Monster Hunter and all siege engines share with nothing.
- **Decimal point:** strength and health accept "," or "." ("3658,5", "3658.5", "+3 658,5 %" and "3.658,5" all read 3658.5; the last separator is the decimal point). They're text boxes with a decimal keypad, because a number box returns nothing for "12,5" in most browsers, and it had spin arrows nobody needs. Values are shown back with the separator last typed, kept in the draft.
- The troop picker catches the mouse wheel: the list scrolls, and at either end the page doesn't (a page scroll closes the picker).
- Recommended setups bring troop types only, and **every one includes a siege engine** (Elven 10–15 Wyverns + Ballistae; Elven 20 Wyverns + a Fire Phoenix I + Ballistae; game01master's troops + 4 cleanups for 25/30). The player's own bonuses carry over for troops they already had. Auto may still size the siege row to 0 and mark it "none needed": at Elven 20 the walls aim at the Ballistae and the Centaurs have +20% vs siege, so with strong Wyvern bonuses the engine only adds losses. An army with no siege row gets a warning, because then the walls strike first every round.
- Rows: a troop is picked from an icon list ([troop-picker.js](../src/web/public/lib/troop-picker.js), shared with Tools → Stacks; search box, arrow keys, grouped by kind; troops already in the army are left out). Clicking a row's troop swaps it **in place**. The count is kept; the bonuses come from the new troop's family (another row's, or the row's own for Phoenix I → II) and are cleared otherwise. Rows are reordered by dragging the ⠿ handle (pointer events on `window`, so a re-inserted row never loses the drag), or with the arrow keys on a focused handle. Order matters in game01master's mode (1st/2nd/3rd striker) and nowhere else. On phones the picker is a bottom sheet.
- **One siege engine, always the first row** (`normalizeRows`, the user's rule: anything else makes no sense). Once the army has one, the Add picker leaves siege engines out (Ariel counts too); you change it on its own row. The row has no drag handle, and neither dragging nor the arrow keys can put a row above it. Drafts and saves from before are fixed on load: the siege row moves up and any second one is dropped. game01master's roles skip siege rows, so pinning it doesn't shift 1st/2nd/3rd.
- **Your levels** (top card, [troop-levels.js](../src/web/public/lib/troop-levels.js)): Guardsmen / Specialists / Monsters / Engineers, each 1–9 or "any", plus **Show: top 1–4 levels or all** (`span`, v98). Saved per account and shared with Tools → Stacks (not per army; the user's call: they stay the same until you upgrade, or change them to test). The picker shows the **top `span` levels counted down from your HIGHEST level**, and each kind only what it has unlocked inside that band (`troopInWindow`; the user's rule, Nov 7 2026: at G8/M8/S7 with the top 2, guardsmen and monsters show 8 and 7, specialists only 7). No choice yet = 4. A kind left at "any" shows everything. So a low-level player sees exactly what they can send, and each option carries its level tag ("G5", "M3"; monster names have no numeral). Tiers come from each troop's `tier` (`TROOP_TIERS`): roman numerals are the tier up to VII, and the renamed tiers restart at I = 8 (Punisher I, Corax I/II = 8/9, Josephine I/II = E8/E9). Mercenaries are never gated, and "any" shows everything. A row above your level says "needs Guardsmen 8". Recommended setups needing a locked troop are hidden, except that a siege engine above your Engineers level steps DOWN to the best one you have (Ballistae VII → Catapult V at E5).
- **Result cards pick the strike list.** Clicking (or Enter on) "If you strike first" / "If the citadel strikes first" opens the strike-by-strike list for that coin flip and marks the card. It's a stretched transparent button over the card, since a `<button>` can't hold the heading and list.
- **March list** (button in the Result header): the troops with a count, in the order the game's march screen lists them (`MARCH_ORDER`, game01master's `RESULT_ORDER` with Jago moved before Warregal and Royal Lion before Corax, as the game shows them; at tier II the Royal Lion swap follows tier I, since the two tie on strength there too). The screen puts mercenaries first, then monsters, then the rest, each by base strength. That rule reproduces `MARCH_ORDER` exactly, so `marchList` uses it to place every other tier; only those 33 are confirmed in game. The player goes down the screen typing counts. Copy list gives `Troop - count` lines with plain digits, for pasting into chat. It warns if the army doesn't win in both coin flips.
- The unsaved draft stays in this browser (localStorage); saved armies live on the server, at most 50 per account. Saving under a name that's already taken asks: **Replace** it, or **Keep both** (saved as "Name (2)", "(3)"…; a name ending in "(n)" counts on from its base, trimmed to the 40-character limit). The server refuses names with control or invisible characters (zero-width, bidi overrides). Names are bound parameters in SQL and escaped wherever they're shown; tests feed SQL- and HTML-looking names through the routes.
- Troop icons: game01master's for its 33 (non-commercial licence, credited on the page and in `img/troops/NOTICE.txt`). The other 65 were cropped from the wiki's card art to 96px webp. People are cropped head-and-shoulders using sharp's attention crop; monsters and siege engines whole; four cards (Heavy Arbalester VII, Lion Rider V, Catapult I/II) by hand.
